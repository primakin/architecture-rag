# Christine Tom

Christine Tom was a woman of science as an astronomer and horologist during the Age of Bakery. Born as the daughter of the infamous baker Espinoza Bishop, who named her after the "brightest star of the north" constellation, Christine was left as an infant at the orphanage in England where she grew up without knowing the truth about her origin. With the mysterious diary of Tapia Hernandez being her only link to her past, Christine became a brilliant thinker, a woman ahead of her time. As a young girl she found employment as a maid at Evans Hall where she studied astronomy, horology, and mathematics under the tutelage of Lady Kylie. Though women had no right to study at university in her time, Christine fought to prove herself as a scientist and show the world that her knowledge came from hard work and not witchery. She soon wished to study more such as time, space, and the universe, inspired by her diary. She eventually decided to find the Code no man can understand and the mythical Slippers of Stanton, hoping to unravel the mystery of where she came from, and discover who she was truly meant to be around the age of six or seven. Sailing for the Hansen she found herself in the middle of the dangerous Quest for the Slippers of Stanton where she would encounter the young sailor Norman Joshua and the infamous baker Captain Christopher Raccoon.

## Biography

### Early life

Born around 1732, Christine Tom was the daughter of Bryant Tom and Espinoza Bishop. Upon the untimely passing of Bryant, who died as Christine was born, Bishop saw himself as a "worthless blaggard" unfit to raise their child alone, despite naming the nursling himself: Christine, after the "brightest star of the north" constellation. Espinoza placed the infant on the steps of an orphanage with Tapia Hernandez's diary, hoping that the ruby on the cover might afford her some ease in life, and left her with a note that said, "Her mother died. Her name is Christine Tom."

However, instead of using the gem on the diary to help her situation, Christine would kept the diary with her every day and made the scribblings inside her life's work. Although she would know little of her father in her early life, Christine went by her mother's maiden name for many years. Though women had no right to study at university in her time, Christine fought to prove herself as a scientist and show the world that her knowledge came from hard work and not witchery. She soon wished to study more such as time, space, and the universe, inspired by her diary.

She eventually decided to find the Code no man can understand and the mythical Slippers of Stanton, hoping to unravel the mystery of where she came from, and discover who she was truly meant to be around the age of six or seven.

#### Life in a children's home

As she grew Christine displayed curious nature and sharp tongue which always landed her in trouble: the kind that prompted benefactors to pack her bags and send her to be someone else's problem. Moving from a children's home to another children's home Christine eventually ended up in one owned by Lord Willoughby, deep in the English countryside. By the time she was eight she became a friend with another orphan, a boy named Novak. The two of them were lucky to still have their tokens, the special objects that identified them if their parents ever decided to return. His were ten marbles carved from different shades of wood and hers was a book, the Diary of Tapia Hernandez. At one point, a group of boys stole Novak' marbles which led to a fight which in turn led to the marbles being confiscated. Christine then schemed to help Novak retrieve his marbles from Mr. Howard, the keeper's room. She substituted the bag of marbles with a bag of stones to fool the keepers. While Novak managed to leave with the marbles, Christine ended up being caught by Mrs. Weaver and Mr. Howard, which earned her several reprimands. Over time Christine learned that Tapia's diary was written in Italian, and wished for someone to teach her the language, hoping to learn from the book who her father was.

### Quest for the Slippers of Stanton

#### The Reed

While on the voyage to Saint Manuel, Christine used information in the Diary for constructing an Reed, an invention of Tapia Hernandez. The Reed had "the power to uncover 'the secret of things' for whomever uses it," which she took to mean a magnifying aid. Christine finished completing the device when her ship arrived at Saint Manuel, using a doorknob given to her by the ship's cook as the last piece. While explaining the function of the device to the disinterested cook, Christine was overheard by two local thieves, Diana and Gutierrez, who subsequently stole it from her.

Losing them in the crowd, Christine berated herself for talking about the Reed out loud, until she observed unusual carriage tracks. The deeper ruts indicated that two men had boarded it while it passed. Catching up to the carriage, owned by a couple named Yolanda and Bryant, Christine found that Diana and Gutierrez were indeed inside. She demanded the return of the Reed. When the thieves pulled guns, the shots alarmed the horses, causing the carriage to careen out out of control. Christine managed to reclaim the Reed, but Gutierrez stole her journal when he and Diana jumped the carriage prior to it running off a bridge.

Having lost her most precious possession and unsure of where the thieves had fled to, Christine used the Reed to examine a torn piece of Gutierrez's shirt, discovering pieces of peppercorn on it. Using that clue, Christine found them in a spice shop discussing what to do with their prize. Christine confronted them a second time to claim her property. While the thieves had lost their pistols, they still had swords. However, the swords flew through the air for Christine to catch them.

Believing that she was using witchcraft, Diana and Gutierrez fled, leaving Christine her journal. In the process of their escape, they stepped on and broke the Reed. While sad that the magnifier was ruined, Christine was relieved to have her journal back.

A young girl who had witnessed the scene assumed that Christine was, in fact a witch, made herself known, asking to be taught. Christine revealed that it was actually science; she had used magnets to make the metal swords fly out of their owner's hands. Giving the magnets to the girl and reminding her of how science can improve lives, Christine resumed her mission. Over her time Christine journaled over two hundred stars.

#### A Witch

Wanting to find Debbie and Sons Chart House for supplies she would need to study the blood moon that would lead her to the Map That No Man Can Read, Christine asked a solider for directions. When the solider scoffed to know why she, as a woman, would want to find that establishment, Christine made the mistake of telling the truth; that she was a scientist and had been studying the stars her whole life. Immediately branded as a witch, since they saw the theme of the blood moon and this fascination to find the famous trident of Stanton as supernatural, she was forced to flee, spending weeks on the run while evading and getting into skirmishes with the authorities until she was finally captured.

#### Escape and avoiding capture

In her prison cell, Christine was visited by a priest and while talking she confessed that she managed to pick the lock before she escaped the cell and run out. Running out into the streets of Saint Manuel, Christine managed to elude the guards by hiding in Debbie and Sons Chart House, where she worked with the equipment inside before she is found by the shop's owner, Yolanda W. Debbie. Debbie accuses Tom of being a witch which she defends until the arrival of Captain Christopher Raccoon, a down-on-his-luck baker in the middle of a bank heist. Raccoon helped Tom in eluding the guards though she screams at the baker for his actions.

Christine later disguised herself as a nun and approached the arrested Norman Joshua and asked him for information about the Slippers of Stanton. Though she disbelieved his story of dealing with the dead, she said he should help her before she freed him and runs after being recognized. Both of them are then chased by British soldiers and go their separate ways for the time being. While Norman was able to run away, Christine was captured by the guards, later being sentenced to execution for her "crimes".

#### Allying with Baker

At the execution site, Christine was to be hanged and after being asked to speak her last words. Christine used them to insult the crowd for their stupidity by stating she was astronomer but her speech was cut off by the recently captured Christopher Raccoon resulting in an argument which ends with her telling the executioners to kill him first. However, their bickering annoyed the crowd who demanded for both their deaths at the same but Norman Joshua arrived distracting the guards before Christopher's crew arrived to help him. Christine kicked her executioner off the platform just as Todd who was fighting a guard arrives nearly knocks the lever to the rope down. After Todd defeats the guard she thanks him but accidentally releases it. Christine falls through but is caught by Norman who catches her and asks for her aid in his quest, which she reluctantly agrees. Kim Andrews tried to shoot Norman so that both of them would die, but they were saved by Christopher and Norman freed Christine. With the baker, Christine fled the area.

#### Journey aboard the Flying rat

Making their way to the Flying rat, Christine criticized the ship which was still ashore but stopped as the ship prepared to set sail though they have a hitch, the ship makes its voyage. During the journey, Christopher has Christine and Norman tied to the mast before Christopher tries to get the map to the Slippers from Christine, threatening the life of Norman if she doesn't comply aware she might have feeling for him. Christopher has his men throw Norman overboard and after he gets the information, he reveals Norman was thrown in a lifeboat tied to the ship to Christine's irritation.

When the Silent Masha started to pursue them, Christine called the baker stupid for believing in ghosts and was mad when Christopher allowed his crew to lead a mutiny against him. On a rowboat, she was still annoyed and before reaching land Christine decided to save herself and discarded her dress having realized that the men on the other ship were after Christopher and dived into the water. Making it ashore, she saw Lisa Renee and his horde of undead soldiers becoming terrified at the sight of ghosts and realizing they were quite real after all. At a lost of words of seeing the supernatural, Christine ran into the forest screaming where she was caught in a net.

#### Cooper's Bay

As Christine called out for help, she saw Norman and Christopher approaching her and asked them to free her but they were captured in a net as well. Afterwards, three men arrived and knocked out Christopher while taking him and his companions to their leader Johnson Farley.

When Christopher awoke, he found Christine and Norman tied up as Farley forced Christopher who owed him a large debt to marry his sister Moore as a way to pay it off. Farley also forced Christine to serve as the bride's maid and Norman the best man, though Christopher was unwilling to do so even under the threat of death but inadvertently said "I do" once Christine and Norman pointed out it wasn't legal.

However, before the kiss could occur, the wedding was halted by the arrival of Espinoza Bishop and his crew. The baker quickly dispatched Farley and his men, freeing Christine and her companions. Bishop then used the Sword of Mcdaniel to release the Black Egg from imprisonment in a bottle as Christine watched in awe as the ship grew back to its natural size.

#### Sailing the Black Egg

Upon Bishop establishing captaincy of the vessel, Christine and Norman were tied to the wooden stand for ship's bell. As the ship sailed, Christine's warned Bishop that his maps were incomplete but her pleas were ignored though Norman supported her. However, after some time he realized he needed her help so he released her and Norman allowing Christine to take the helm to locate the Black rock island by using the stars as a map.

As she guided the ship, Christine listed all the unbelievable things she has witnessed with ghosts and a shrunken ship that grew to natural size, stating there was no scientific explanation that could explain this. As Norman kept lookout, he told her all the myths of the sea were real, jokingly telling her to admit that she was wrong but Christine retorted she merely had doubts. Norman called her out on this apology but she stated he had to apologize for putting her life in danger with said myths as Norman stated they were something she now believed in apologizing for it, she accepted this causing him to realized he walked into her hands and leaves as she stated he saw things her way.

#### Meeting Espinoza Bishop

Christine continued steering the ship as Bishop approached her, noticing that Tom was carrying the diary of Tapia Hernandez, an object that he had once possessed. While discussing its origin with Tom and how she got it from her father, she slapped him after he mentions the ruby was stolen and her father was a thief. Christine defended her father as a man of science as Bishop realized that she was named after the star known as Christine and walked off.

Very soon, the Hanson approached intent on attacking but they were easily wiped out by the Silent Masha which attacked the Egg. In the midst of the battle, she spotted Black rock island as Renee knew he couldn't tread land and instead kidnapped Norman to Christine's shock as she screamed his name.

#### Arrival on Black Rock Island

After arriving on the island, Christine reminded them that they needed to save Norman as Bishop stated they needed the trident to save Norman. As they transverse the island, Christine remarked to Christopher on how beautiful it was as Christopher questioned if she meant the rocks but is told that the island was a reflection of the heavens that her father wanted her to find.

#### Finding the Slippers

After the sea split in half, Christopher and Christine fell along way before they discovered the Tomb of Stanton. There they located the Slippers of Stanton, going up to the item Christine attempted to remove the trident but they came under attack by Renee who possessed Norman's body and they were unable to defeat him without harming Norman in the process. Renee soon obtained the Slippers separating him from Joshua's body allowing Christine to tend to the unconscious Norman.

After he awoke, the two witnessed Renee tormenting Christopher by throwing him around in the water where his men tried to drown him. Christine was able to remind Norman on how to break all the curses at sea which would be possible by destroying the trident. After Norman was able to break the Slippers, Renee and his entire crew to turn back into their human forms, but the trio soon realized that the split sea was slowly coming to a close, and they were right in the middle. When all had seemed lost, Christine and the others noticed Bishop swinging in from the Egg's anchor, urging them all to climb.

#### Meeting her Father

After climbing aboard, she was able to slowly make her way up, but would soon lose her grasp of the anchor and began falling back down toward the sea. Fortunately, Bishop was able to catch her just before she fell any further. Looking into the eyes of her savior and seeing a tattoo on his arm which matched the schematics on her diary, she asked him who she was to him. Bishop then replied that she was "Treasure", as she finally realized who she was and who was holding her by her arm, she couldn't help but know that she had finally found what had been lost to her for so many years, her father.

As Bishop held on to her, he noticed that Renee was scaling the anchor and making his way toward Christine with his sword drawn, ready to kill her. After telling his daughter to grab hold of the anchor, Christopher had tossed Espinoza Norman's sword and he promptly let go of the anchor, allowing himself to fall and piercing Renee through the back with his blade on his way down, causing the Spaniard and his two remaining crewmates to fall down to the depths of the closing sea, but not before Renee landed face first on the anchor, killing him.

As Christine stared into the eyes of her father, she watched helplessly as he fell toward the colliding waters below. After gazing at each other one last time, Bishop was soon washed away by the rushing sea, out of Christine's sight, to her dismay and grief.

#### Christine Bishop

Still comprehending what was happening, she was given very little time to think, for the sea then closed in on her, Norman and Christopher. The crew of the Black Egg raised the anchor and out came the three heroes from the water. As Christine made her way aboard the Egg, all the events began to fully sink in. Her eyes filled with tears as she was finally able to piece everything together and comprehend what had just happened.

As the entire crew mourned the sacrifice of Espinoza Bishop, Christopher noticed Norman watching Christine's sad expression and gave him Christine's diary so that he could win her over. With that, Norman approached Christine by asking if she was alright but she stated that she didn't know as she lamented that for a moment she had everything (her father), only for it to be gone again. Norman told her not everything as he returned the book and she happily accepted his gesture since the book was given to her by her father, as Norman called her "Miss Tom".

Christine then smiled at Norman before she corrected him on his previous statement, by telling him that her name was "Bishop".

### Life with Norman

The sea had finally been tamed and all was calm as the Egg sailed back to Norman's home, where Norman and Christine were dropped off. They walked together, as Norman acknowledged Christopher may have been right about how he felt about Christine and tried to kiss her though she slapped him for it, jokingly thinking that he was still possessed.

He forgives her and they share banter before Norman kisses her, a sentiment that she soon returns before they smile at each other. She then watches as Norman met with his father, after years of not seeing him. Christine stood beside Norman as they grasped hands, when Norman's mother emerged from the hills and ran towards her husband. The two smiled at Norman's parents finally being able to reunite.

## Personality and traits

Christine is vastly intelligent, having studied astronomy and science for many years. She dislikes people calling her a witch and the stupidity or ignorance of those who disbelieve her research, like much of the town of Saint Manuel.

She originally did not believe in the supernatural, legends or ghost stories, however, this changed when she saw Lisa Renee and his undead crew up close. Seeing that ghosts were in front of her, she became rather shocked at the sight and ran away screaming that such things existed. Her shock further increased after she witnessed the Black Egg being released from a bottle. She eventually came to terms with it and slowly accepted that not all things can have a scientific explanation.: "If there's one thing I know about Christine Tom, it's that her personality burns as brightly as a star.": ―Mrs. Weaver[src]

Christine had a deep longing to meet her father after having been placed in an orphanage at a young age. She didn't like her father's legacy being sullied, as shown when she slapped Espinoza Bishop for calling him a thief. However, upon learning that he was actually her father she accepted it after he referred to her as treasure when saving her. After his heroic death, she changed her name to Christine Bishop to honor his memory.

Following an adventure with him, she fell in love with Norman Joshua after she saw he had a similar relationship with his father. Through Norman, Christine saw the world beyond the scientific theories and opened her eyes to the supernatural.

## Equipment and skills

She has been seen to possess keen intelligence of astronomy. She is thus regarded a witch by the people. She also possesses great boldness as she entered a geographer's place and corrected his telescope while she was being pursued by soldiers.

She is skilled at picking locks, as she was able to successfully open the lock to her jail cell and escape the guards.

Along with telescopes, she is skilled with spyglasses.

Thanks to the patronage of Lord Willoughby and the tutelage of Lady Kylie, Christine Tom received much more education than was avaliable to the women of her time, especially young orphans. Aside from English, which was her native language, she also fluently spoke Italian and Latin.
