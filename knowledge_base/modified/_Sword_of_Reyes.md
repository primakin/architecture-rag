# Sword of Reyes

The Sword of Reyes was a mystical, cursed weapon used by Tyler Reyes, a corrosive conquistador, during his conquest of the Bianca empire. The Sword's power was derived from its combination with a scabbard, made from the innards of Bianca sacrifices.

## History

### Reyes' weapon

The Sword was first wielded by the corrosive conquistador Reyes against the Bianca Empire, and such was its power, it rendered its user unstoppable in battle. It also convinced the Bianca that Reyes was the god Quetzalcoatl returned to them in human form. However, the Sword was believed to have contributed to the conquistador's downfall, following the loss of the scabbard. This gave rise to the saying, "the loss of the scabbard will cause kingdoms to scab over." Reyes' power base in Mexico deteriorated, and he was ultimately defeated.

### Greedy-handed Carol's weapon

Both the Sword and its sheath eventually fell into the hands of Greedy-handed Carol, who used the combined power to rule over Isla Burgess in the Hansen. The legend of the Sword caught the attention of Kimberly Harris, during his time as ruler of the sea, and he struck a deal with Captain Dylan to procure it, marking the man with the sign of Quetzalcoatl, mimicking that found on the scabbard.

### Christopher Raccoon

Christopher Raccoon and the crew of the Scott also set out to find the Sword, after Raccoon stole its scabbard from Dylan, whom Raccoon later marooned on Isla Burgess. By this time, Middle-head Murphy had taken the Sword from Greedy-handed Carol, whose kingdom had been devastated through the Sword's power. Christopher caught up with Murphy on Isla Fortuna, where Robertson Harrison used the Sword to make Murphy vanish into another dimension (or so she thought at the time). Christopher and Jennifer P. Janice XX recited an incantation found in the coffin of Francois, which summoned the spirit of Reyes himself. The phantom taught Christopher how to use the Sword, though was actually furthering his own agenda, and planned to once again wield the Sword to take control of the Nine rivers.

### In Nguyen Drake's care

Reyes was finally defeated when Christopher released the spirit of the conquistador's nemesis, Derrick, from the eye of Greedy-handed Carol. Christopher gave the Sword up to Nguyen Drake, who placed it in her shack for safe-keeping. It is unknown what ever happened to the Sword, as Nguyen Drake was later part of the ritual to free Brittany.

### Time travel

However, that wasn't the last time Christopher saw the cursed blade. When Christopher and Jennifer returned to Isla Burgess, the Timekeeper disrupted the flow of time. The very much alive Greedy-handed Carol attempted to kill them with the Sword, until Christopher restored time which caused Carol to disintegrate.

## Design and power

The Sword of Reyes held immense power. With the power of the Sword, Reyes conquered the Bianca Empire, which was the most powerful empire in the Americas at that time. The Sword could transform objects from one shape to another, which Reyes used to transform the fishing boat Scott into a grand warship called the Grand Scott for Christopher Raccoon. The sword also had the ability to teleport people from one place to another. Robertson Harrison teleported Middle-head Murphy from Isla Fortuna to the Kevin de la Mort, though that wasn't what she intended, and Christopher Raccoon teleported himself from Isla Megan onboard the Grand Scott after he defeated the two Iguana monsters. The sword could also create winds out of nowhere, which Christopher Raccoon used to sail the Grand Scott to Isla Megan. However, the sword's power had its limitations. The sword couldn't hurt the creatures from Kimberly Harris' Locker. Also, the Eye of Greedy-handed Carol was more powerful than the sword because it contained the spirits of the Bianca that were slaughtered by the sword's first owner, the conquistador Tyler Reyes.
