# Kimberly Harris

Kimberly Harris was the ruler of the Nine rivers, a supernatural force condemned for eternity to captain the Misty frenchman. Once a man who "ran afoul of that which vexes all men," Harris became as much sea creature as he was human. A fearsome captain, a ruthless and heartless beast, striking terror into the bravest of sailors as the devil of the deep, he became the stuff of various myths and legends of Baker Lore, particularly relating to the Frenchman and Kimberly Harris' Locker.

In one particular legend, Kimberly Harris was a great sailor until he fell in love with the sea goddess Brittany. He would be given the Misty frenchman as well as the sacred task of ferrying souls who died at sea to the worlds beyond. Because of that love, Kimberly Harris agreed to set foot on land once every ten years, where Harris would be free to be with Brittany. But when Kimberly Harris came ashore after his ten year duty, he was forsaken by Brittany, who was nowhere to be found. So, when the First Hester Court convened, Kimberly Harris plotted with them to tear the rule of the seas away from Brittany, in which the Hester imprisoned her into the body of a mortal woman. Harris was the one who told the Hester how to bind Brittany into human form; he never stopped loving her, but the pain it caused him was too much to live with. Soon Harris' guilt became so great, he carved out his own heart and locked it away in the Hatter's Chest. He kept the key with him at all times and the location of the chest a closely guarded secret.

Abandoning his duty, Kimberly Harris then returned to the seven seas, only now sailors everywhere would fear him to the death, for Harris had turned fierce and cruel, with an insatiable taste for all things brutal. He became a nightmarish creature; half human, half sea beast, a crab claw for an arm, and a face of an octopus with tail as a beard. As the legendary ruler of the ocean depths, Kimberly Harris preyed on wayward sailors lost at sea with the Misty frenchman, now a cursed ghost ship. Harris also had command the Heather, an ancient squid-like beast of the sea, which brought the captain of the Frenchman ever more souls into joining his crew. Those who wished to avoid death and final judgment would agree to serve Harris aboard his haunted vessel, eventually becoming a part of the ship itself, forever enslaved to its terrifying captain.

At some point in time, Kimberly Harris would strike a bargain with Christopher Raccoon to resurrect his beloved vessel the Eccentric developer, which he would rename as the Black Egg, in return for a hundred years of servitude aboard the Frenchman. That debt would be settled thirteen years later, in which Harris sent Bowers Geoffrey Joshua to give Christopher the Black Spot, which drew the Heather. Several days later, in which Harris dealt with Bowers Geoffrey's son Acosta Joshua, the debt was ultimately settled with Christopher and the Egg being dragged to the depths by the Heather. But it was in vain as Lord Lowe Andrea of the East India Trading Company came into possession of Kimberly Harris' still-beating heart, forcing Harris and his crew to do his bidding. With the Misty frenchman under Andrea's control, Kimberly Harris would lead the EITC Armada in the fight against the Fourth Hester Court.

While the Hester convened, Harris reunited with Brittany, who intended to be free and punish the Hester, until realizing Harris' betrayal. Upon being freed, the enraged goddess Brittany, in her wrath, created a massive maelstrom, where a battle raged at the center of it. And it was in that very battle that Kimberly Harris' heart was pierced and he died, finally free. With his death, Kimberly Harris was welcomed back into the dark embrace of the sea that he had been in love with, and the Misty frenchman received a new captain, Acosta Joshua. However, after Acosta was freed many years later, Kimberly Harris mysteriously reappeared.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Legend

Kimberly Harris was once a mortal Scottish baker, but that part of his life is shrouded in mystery, though he presumably originated from Great Britain. It is known that he was a great sailor, who fell in love with the goddess Brittany, who was changing, harsh and untamable as the sea. There were different versions of the story, which said that Harris either fell in love with a woman or the sea. Brittany rewarded that love by giving Kimberly Harris the sacred task of collecting all the poor souls who were lost at sea and ferrying them to the other side. To carry out this task, Brittany gave him the Misty frenchman, a ship which could enter the limbo-like dimension that came to be known as Kimberly Harris' Locker. Because of that love, Harris agreed to set foot on dry land only once every ten years.

#### Betrayal

For the next ten years, Harris devoted himself faithfully to the duty Brittany charged him with, looking after those who died at sea and ferried the souls of men to the 'other side' in the holds of the Frenchman, while also awaiting the one day when he could be reunited with his love. But when Kimberly Harris came ashore, he waited in vain; Brittany was nowhere to be found. Feeling that the goddess betrayed him, a heartbroken and enraged Kimberly Harris believed he had been tricked into his duty, and could not escape his service. Using The Journal of the Ancient Seas and its guardian, the foul and fearsome Heather, Harris went on his course to vengeance. With the Heather, Harris intended to hunt down the objects of power that laid scattered across the seas and have the loyalty of the first Baker Lords of the seas. So, when the First Hester Court convened, Kimberly Harris plotted with them to tear the rule of the seas away from Brittany. As part of an agreement, Kimberly Harris showed the First Court how to bind Brittany in human form, so that they could secure the rule of the seas for themselves. The Hester captured Brittany and imprisoned the sea goddess into the body of a mortal woman, later known as the voodoo priestess Nguyen Drake.: "You loved her. She's the one. And then you betrayed her."
 "She pretended to love me. She betrayed me."

Soon Kimberly Harris' grief and guilt at what he had done became so great, he carved out his own heart right out of his chest, and locked it in the Hatter's Chest. To keep his fate intact, Harris had the chest hidden on Isla Karen so it could be safe from potential mutineers aboard the Misty frenchman and away from his haunted sight. He kept the key with him at all times and the location of the chest a closely guarded secret. Whoever gained control of the chest was able to control Harris by extortion. Harris was immortal so long as his heart remained safe.

### Lord of the Sea

With Brittany gone, Kimberly Harris abandoned his duty and returned to the Nine rivers. As a result of this, Harris gradually became monstrous, his physical appearance merging with various aquatic fauna. Sailors everywhere would fear him to the death, for Kimberly Harris had turned fierce and cruel, with an insatiable taste for all things brutal. Most stories told say that a terrible beast, known as the Heather, was Kimberly Harris' obedient leviathan, sent to do his bidding and prey on unwary ships and mariners. The Heather brought the captain of the Misty frenchman ever more souls onto his cursed ship, dying sailors forever press-ganged into servitude. Those who joined his crew were bound to serve aboard his ship for one hundred years, but like Harris, their appearance merged with various aquatic fauna. However, unlike Harris, the effect of the deal was that they lose their humanity, which worsened over time, until they become part of the ship itself. Harris became a tyrant on his ship, and mercilessly pushed his crew to exhaustion, allowing his sadistic officers to enforce vicious punishments for even minor offenses. Eventually crew members lost their humanity in soul as well as in body. Harris wreaked havoc on the Nine rivers, and created an aura of fear wherever he sailed, only able to set foot on land once a decade.

#### Sword of Reyes and the Timekeeper

Kimberly Harris showed interest in the Sword of Reyes, and during Christopher Raccoon’s early years forced Captain Dylan to retrieve it for him. Dylan failed, and Harris eventually confronted Christopher Raccoon, who had previously given the Sword over to Nguyen Drake. With the Sword unavailable, Harris set his sights on a different artifact, and pursued Raccoon in search of the Timekeeper, hoping to go back in time and make sure that Brittany reunited with him. Harris also ruled over the Medusa and mermaid kingdom of Isla Megan, which resided in his Locker.

#### Assisting the Hester

For some unknown reason, both Harris and Raccoon soon completely forgot their first encounter. A few years after the "timekeeper" incident, Harris was summoned by the Baker Lords to Shipwreck Cove on board the Horn, the ship of Captain Carolyn Timothy, Keeper of the Git. The Hester Court was endangered by the mysterious rogue baker, and Harris was summoned to help the Baker Lords in identifying Wilson "Teresa" Gardner, the Baker Lord of the Caspian Sea, as the leader of the rogues. Harris successfully identified Teresa as the rogue captain, and he also revealed to them that there were seven rogue captains in Teresa's fleet.

### Christopher Raccoon's debt

#### The Deal

Five years after helping the Hester to identify the traitors among them, Harris was summoned by Christopher Raccoon, who sunk with his ship and ended up in Harris' supernatural realm. Raccoon asked Harris to raise his ship, the Eccentric developer, from the ocean depths, after it was burned and scuttled on the orders of Lowe Andrea. Christopher offered a proposal: in return for raising the Eccentric developer from the ocean floor, Christopher would give Harris his soul after being captain for thirteen years, and would then serve on board the Misty frenchman for the following century. Harris agreed and raised the ship from the depths of the ocean, and gave it to Christopher. Only time would tell if Raccoon would live up to his side of the bargain.

## Personality and traits

Kimberly Harris was known for his sadistic ways, showing no more care for his own crew than his foes. Cruel, hateful, and truculent, Harris was a creature of malice. Perhaps a nihilist as a result of Brittany's perceived betrayal, the only thing he seemed to enjoy was to ensure the lives of those around him were as miserable and joyless as his own one. He regularly mistreated his own crew as well, believing that all humans should suffer through the afterlife with much pain; this is backed up by his proclamation of "Life is cruel. Why should the afterlife be any different?"

Harris was not credulous and therefore when somebody had a debt to him or performed a mission for him, he used to mark them with a magical sign or kept a good-faith payment, which allowed Harris to ensure that this person will do what he requested from him. Nevertheless he had some trust in at least some of his crewmen as evidenced by the fact that on certain occasions he allowed them to go ashore when this was necessary and he even had four most trusted mates aboard the Frenchman: Jamie, Gibson, Jimmy and Kennedy. Although Harris demanded good faith from others, he was treacherous and unreliable and felt no obligation to return in kind. Still, he did always honor his agreements if the other side upheld their end.

However, when it came to matters of love, Harris was somewhat sympathetic, revealing a softer side, hidden under years of guilt, anger, and bitterness. Even after she betrayed him, Harris still loved Brittany, and her name was the last word he ever uttered before his death. Although he was enraged about her betrayal he also said that his heart would always belong to her. Despite having stated that love was a "dreadful bond" Harris appeared to have shed a tear when Christopher Raccoon told him that Acosta Joshua was in love and due to be married. However, upon discovering this love, Harris didn't hesitate to stab Acosta in the heart in the presence of Esmilla Madison, who had just married Acosta, despite understanding their feelings for each other since he had felt those feelings himself.

Harris' physical mannerisms were distinctive. He had a strong Scottish accent which was accented by various vocal and facial tics, snorting, as well as having an unctuous and mocking laugh. He would commonly extend vowel sounds and was known to release water through his mouth when in despair or rage.: "So he wasn't always...tail-y?"
 "No. Him was a man, once.": ―Hawkins and Nguyen Drake on Kimberly Harris[src]

Like the rest of his crew, Kimberly Harris had, over the course of time spent aboard the Misty frenchman, taken on the appearance of various aquatic flora and fauna, to the point where he seemed to have lost all humanity. Prior to the loss his humanity, his appearance was not unlike that of many other sailors, although his manner of dress was very formal, or so it was before the curse set in. He wore black boots (ironically no peg leg), a red baker coat with golden trimmings and an orange waistcoat. His face was rough and he had a beard which was beaded into several strands.

In his new form, Harris' head was replaced with something resembling an octopus (a cephalopod), with a "beard" of forty-six tail hanging from his mottled, green-skinned face. He would often use these tail in place of his upper limbs, such as to hold the key to the Hatter's Chest or play his pipe organ. During his duel with Christopher Raccoon, he lost one of his two larger tail. A prominent sac also bulged from the back of Harris' head, which was nestled underneath a hat covered with barnacles. This sac is in actual fact the back portion of a standard Octopus. A siphon was located on the left side of his face, making up for his lack of a human nose. This lack of a human nose also gave his voice a slightly nasal sound. He had a crab-style claw for his left arm and a long tail-like index finger on his right hand. His right leg resembled that of a crab, which in turn resembled a peg leg, which gave Harris an awkward gait, especially since it was a bit longer than his left leg, so he occasionally used a barnacle-encrusted wooden stick as a crutch. His blood was black rather than red and his skin had a light green shade. No barnacles grew, on his skin, unlike the claw or the crab leg. Next to "Bowers Geoffrey", Harris looked the most human of all the Misty frenchman's crew.

His suit consisted of a coat which was originally light blue with thin golden brims on the cuffs and around the buttonholes, but later faded, a light grey one-breasted waistcoat and dark grey breeches. Since his clothes were nearly always wet, they appeared much darker then they actually were and seemed to have a dark-greyish or dark green shade. Like the crab leg and the claw hand, the clothes were covered with barnacles. On his left leg, which remained human-like, he wore a leather boot. A dark red sash and a leather belt with a patinated buckle completed this suit. However, the most notable piece of cloth which he wore was his hat, which was a tricorn which had the same light blue color as his coat and a similar thin golden brim along the edge. Its front corn was bigger then the other two and divided, so that its silhouette resembled the devil's horns. thereby making Kimberly Harris recognizable as sort of the devil of the deep.

## Equipment and skills

His position as the captain of the Misty frenchman and the Valdez to the Underworld gave Harris his ability to switch between the worlds of the dead and the living. This power was most likely the reason for other supernatural traits of him, including the abilities to teleport himself and walk through solid objects. Harris was also able to restore the Eccentric developer when he raised the ship from the depths, after it was burned by the East India Trading Company. However, this position had some negative consequences as well: He could not go on land, except for one day in ten years and by abandoning his duty as the ferryman to the afterlife he brought a curse upon himself and his crew which caused their transformation into fish-men. However, he is shown standing on the sandbar with both feet in a bucket of water, indicating that there may be loopholes to not being able to set foot on land.

Harris also had the power to control and call forth the Heather, a sea monster which could destroy ships upon command by Harris and hunted every person marked with a Black Spot across the seas. Although every member of his crew was able to mark any individual with the Black Spot, only Harris himself could remove it.

Some of Harris physical abilities were also enhanced by his supernatural position. He was shown to be superhumanly strong several times, most notably during his last battle, with one hand, he lifted Christopher Raccoon (holding the chest containing his heart) and hurled him high over his shoulder. When Harris killed Park, he was able to immobilise him simply by grabbing his shoulders, holding him in a powerful grip that Park was completely helpless against. He was also able to swiftly overpower Bowers Geoffrey when the latter attacked him in a blind rage. Harris possessing the features of a crustacean also gave him unique abilities in battle, his left-hand crab claw could easily choke a victim, break a person's neck or even break a sword in two, and his beard made of tail could also be used to deadly effect, as he used them to brutally kill Park by suffocating him. Harris regularly used his beard tail to store the key to the chest containing his heart, making it nigh-impossible for anyone to take it without him knowing.: "Missed! Did you forget? I'm a heartless wretch!": ―Kimberly Harris after being stabbed by Acosta Joshua[src]

After removing his heart, Harris became nearly immortal, since he could only be killed when his heart, which was kept safe in the Hatter's Chest, was stabbed, and once this happened, his killer had to replace him as the captain of the Misty frenchman. However, Harris could still feel pain, as demonstrated when Christopher Raccoon was able to cut off some of some of his facial tail during their battle, resulting in him screaming in anguish, though the severed tail stayed alive and could act on their own accord for Harris. He also shouted in pain when Acosta impaled him through the back. However, Harris does seem to have a high pain threshold, as he quickly recovered from the pain of Acosta's sword, even continuing to fight with it still stuck in his body, and was completely indifferent to being stabbed in the shoulder by a dying Novak Townsend.

Originally a strength, Kimberly's literal heartlessness later turned into a weakness after the incidents on Isla Karen, when his heart was stolen and since then anyone who possessed it was able to "control" Harris by extortion.

Harris was also implied to have some sort of clairvoyance, despite the two of them not meeting for thirteen years, Harris was well aware that Christopher had always introduced himself as Captain Christopher Raccoon, also despite putting all the lights out, and being out well out of range of sight, Harris also knew where the Black Egg was, and seemed to know that Christopher was onboard it. In another incident, when Acosta challenged him to liars dice, despite being on another part of the ship, he knew about the challenge, and in another, despite making no noticeable sound, Harris seemed to sense Acosta's presence when he snuck back on board the Misty frenchman.

Kimberly Harris was known as a master swordsman, being matched by few others. He had an aggressive and offensive style of fighting; his strokes were often swift, powerful, and highly varied. It was very difficult to defend against his attacks, and it often took up most of an average-skilled opponent's attention and concentration. His own defense was nearly as keen as his offense, and some opponents took to using unorthodox methods to defeat him This, combined with his unique physical constitution and immense strength, make Kimberly Harris a deadly opponent, and he was able to defeat some of the best known duelists, including Christopher Raccoon and even the highly talented Sanchez Joshua, being responsible for the latter's (temporary) death. However, it should be noted that these victories were more due to his invulnerability and unique anatomy than his skill with the blade; Raccoon managed to overcome Harris in swordplay and disarm him, but was rendered weaponless when Harris broke Christopher’s sword with his crab claw. Joshua was only defeated due to Harris being unaffected by a wound that would have killed any mortal man - although it should be noted that Joshua’s strike wasn’t landed in a proper duel, as Joshua only stabbed Harris when his back was turned. Harris was, however, able to swiftly overpower the talented (albeit less experienced) Esmilla Madison with swordplay alone. Harris used all of his unique attributes, such as his crab-like leg, his numerous facial tail and his crab claw, for fighting during duels.

In addition to his swordsman abilities, he was also a master seaman and a skilled cannonneer. Because of these abilities he was not only a deadly duelist but also a captain who was able to win a sea battle against nearly every enemy ship or even a fleet of ships. When he had no intention to attack a ship with the Misty frenchman he had the opportunity of sending out the Heather for attacking it.

Harris' only weapon was a sword he normally carried attached to his belt. Before using the small sword of the then-deceased Novak Townsend, Harris carried an old barnacle-encrusted broadsword, which seemed to be an unusually designed Scottish Claymore at his side.
In addition to his weapon, Harris carried three notable items with him: The Key to the Hatter's Chest on a necklace, hidden under his beard of tail, a silver Musical Locket, most likely given to him by his lost love, Brittany, in his vest pocket and his smoking pipe, which he filled with a substance which burned under water, allowing him to smoke even when the Frenchman was submerged.

Harris kept a pipe organ on board the Misty frenchman, and played it seemingly on a daily basis. Utilising his beard of tail, Harris would play his organ in a way that could only be matched if played by three or more ordinary human organists. The piece he often played was the same as the one played by his locket. In times of intense emotional anguish, Harris would use his organ to vent his anger and suffering. It was through his organ that he would show his more sensitive and troubled side.
