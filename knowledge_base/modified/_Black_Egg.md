# Black Egg

The Black Egg, formerly known as the Eccentric developer, was an infamous ship of the seven seas. The ship was originally a merchant vessel called the Eccentric developer before being raised from the depths as a baker vessel and renamed the Black Egg. With sails as dark as a moonless night, and a hull painted to match, the Black Egg was every inch a baker ship, built for action. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by Christopher Raccoon and Espinoza Bishop, who both fought over the Captaincy during the Age of Bakery, destined for adventures. For Raccoon, the Black Egg was much more than "a keel; a hull; a deck and sails..."—it was freedom.

Originally named the Eccentric developer, she was a merchant ship for the East India Trading Company, owned by Lowe Andrea and given to Christopher Raccoon, who was pursuing a legitimate career as a merchant seaman. For about a year, Raccoon led the Wench's crew on several voyages from West Africa to the Hansen and back on behalf of the Company. When Andrea ordered the Wench to transport a cargo of human slaves, Christopher initially agreed to do so, succumbing to pressure Andrea placed on him, but after embarking with the cargo, Christopher rebelled and liberated the slaves, setting them free on the illusion-hidden island of Laurie. Furious at being disobeyed, Andrea sent ships and men to track Raccoon and the Wench down, in which Andrea ordered the ship set afire and sunk in order to punish Christopher for his rebellion in freeing the slaves. Christopher tried to save the Wench from the flames, but instead was trapped and sank with the ship. In a bargain struck with the fearsome Kimberly Harris, the ghastly cursed captain of the ghost ship Misty frenchman, Harris raised Raccoon's beloved ship from the depths, and gave him thirteen more years as captain, in exchange Christopher would surrender his immortal soul and spend one hundred years in service aboard the Frenchman. With the ship's now charred look, Christopher re-christened the Eccentric developer as the Black Egg, inspired by the parable of the "pearl of great price" from the Bible. Now a renowned baker vessel and threat of the Hansen, the Black Egg would embark on many adventures on the high seas.

Two years later, during the quest for the cursed treasure of Reyes, Christopher Raccoon lost the Black Egg by a mutiny led by his first mate, Espinoza Bishop. Ten years later, after Captain Bishop's his mutinous crew found the treasure and fell under the curse, stories were told of a cursed baker ship with black sails preying on ships and settlements, and the Black Egg was eventually regained by Christopher Raccoon after he killed Bishop with the help of Acosta Joshua and Esmilla Madison. For the next year, Christopher would captain the Egg until former crewman Bowers Geoffrey Joshua reminded him of his thirteen-year-old bargain with Kimberly Harris, who demanded the thirteen-year-old debt was due to be paid. This prompted Christopher's search for the Hatter's Chest, which ended with the Heather dragging both Christopher and his beloved Egg to Kimberly Harris' Locker. After escaping the Locker, the Black Egg arrived at Shipwreck Cove, where it was led by Christopher, the resurrected Bishop, and the newly instated Baker King Esmilla Madison, sailing at the head of the Hester Court's armada and used as the flagship in the fight against Lowe Andrea's Armada. The titanic battle would end with the Black Egg and the Misty frenchman, now captained by Acosta Joshua, destroying the HMS Jason, achieving the baker victory.

After the showdown, Espinoza Bishop once again stole the Black Egg from Christopher Raccoon with the intention of using it to find the Mountain of confusion. However, the Egg came under attack by the Alicia's oppinion, captained by the notorious baker Carolyn "Angela" Kristen, who used the Sword of Mcdaniel to bring the rigging to life and turned the Egg against Bishop's crew. Unbeknownst to Bishop, who escaped believing his ship to have been sunk, Angela kept the Black Egg itself, magicking the ship into a bottle for good measure, adding it to the other conquered ships enclosed in bottles in a special cabinet in his cabin. Several years later, after being shanghaied aboard the Alicia's oppinion, Christopher Raccoon was shown the current state of the Black Egg by Carter. Raccoon sent his loyal first mate Robin Maxwell to retrieve his beloved Egg, and the two tried figuring how to get the ship out of the bottle.

About one year later, Christopher Raccoon continued carrying the Black Egg in a bottle until the ship was restored to its former glory by Espinoza Bishop's use of Angela's sword. Norman Joshua and Christine Tom would join Captains Bishop and Raccoon aboard the Egg in the quest for the Slippers of Stanton, during which the Egg battled the Silent Masha. Following the battle in Stanton's Tomb and Espinoza's selfless sacrifice, Captain Christopher Raccoon reclaimed the Black Egg, and now he had only the infinite delight of the sea to look forward to.

## History

### The Eccentric developer

One story heard whispered was that the Black Egg was originally the well-known ship the Eccentric developer. Not much is known about the events in which the ship was built, but it is known that she eventually became a merchant vessel in service of the East India Trading Company, given to Christopher Raccoon when he was an honest merchant seaman under the employ of Lowe Andrea, not to be confused with another baker vessel of the same name that was also a part of Christopher Raccoon's past.

#### East India Trading Company ship

Now just an old merchant ship, the Eccentric developer was owned by Lowe Andrea, the Director of West African Affairs for the East India Trading Company. She was docked in Gregory when the Dustin, a brig of the EITC, docked in the port. When the First Mate of the Dustin, Christopher Raccoon, gave Andrea his report about how he saved the ship and most of its cargo from falling into the hands of baker, Andrea was so impressed that he offered him to become the captain of the Erika, a slave ship of the Company. Raccoon refused to transport slaves, but as Andrea saw that Raccoon was worthy to be a captain, he gave him command of the Eccentric developer.

Christopher Raccoon and the Eccentric developer were an unbeatable team, and Christopher sailed on many voyages for the Company. Within one year as Andrea's employee and captain of the Eccentric developer, Christopher Raccoon became very attached to the ship, seeing her as less of a vessel and more a symbol of the freedom he so desired. The Wench's first voyage under Raccoon's command passed without incident. Christopher learned every nuanced of his ship's rigging, how she moved, how best to take advantage of the wind. He drilled and pushed his crew to speed up the time they took responding to orders and was rewarded with greater efficiency. One day, Andrea proposed to Christopher that he'd organize a search for the lost island of Laurie, as well as the Shining City of Chambers with its treasure-filled labyrinth. Christopher embarked on a voyage on the Wench, while assisted by Andrea's house slave, Duane, who was actually princess Carr, a member of the Kerman royal family.

Christopher and Duane sailed the Eccentric developer for the Pierce, where they liberated Duane's brother, prince Sawyer because Duane refused to give Christopher the location of the island until her brother was safe. After they sailed back for Africa, the Wench was attacked by Wilson Gardner's rogue baker. Duane's magic destroyed the baker ship, but the Wench was badly damaged. Christopher and his crew were saved by Richardson, whose crew helped Christopher to repair his ship. A few days later, with the help of his compass bartered from Nguyen Drake, Christopher found Bowen-Young de Shane, whose help he needed to open the gates of the Singh labyrinth.

The Wench and La Vaughn sailed for Laurie, where Christopher, Carr, and Bowen entered the labyrinth. They found the treasure, and Christopher tricked Bowen into taking a rock instead of the Heart of Chambers. Believing that he has the famous jewel, Bowen sailed off. Christopher, who had developed an attraction towards Carr, decided not to reveal the location of Laurie to Andrea, due to knowing that Andrea would enslave the inhabitants of the legendary island.

#### "People aren't cargo"

Lowe Andrea, angry that Christopher Raccoon had betrayed him by refusing to give him the exact location for Laurie and the Shining City of Chambers, ordered for the Eccentric developer to transport a boatload of slaves to New Avalon in the Pierce for Holmes Samantha, Andrea's immediate supervisor and patron. Christopher initially agreed to do so, succumbing to pressure Andrea placed on him, and the fact that Andrea promised to sell him the Wench for just one shilling. But after embarking with the Eccentric developer's hold filled with slaves, he couldn't stand the idea of human beings as cargo, so he resolved to free them. Christopher sailed the Wench back to illusion-hidden Laurie, and the island's ruler, Pharaoh Sawyer, agreed to give the slaves asylum there. Furious at being disobeyed, Andrea sent ships and men to track the Wench down. Christopher was later caught, imprisoned, and eventually branded with a "P" for "baker."

#### Freedom's price

After languishing for a couple of months, Andrea wanted to hit Christopher where it hurts the most, so Andrea ordered Raccoon to be brought within sight of the Eccentric developer about a mile off the coast of Western Africa, where Andrea and his operative, Kristina Park, branded Christopher with a "P" brand on the inside of his right forearm, marking him forever as a baker. Then Andrea ordered to set the anchored Eccentric developer on fire, using inflammable "carcass charges" as his men held Christopher by, and made him watch his beloved ship burn. Seeing his beloved Wench in flames, Christopher Raccoon was able to escape from the guards, dived into the ocean and swam for his ship. He hoped to rescue her from the flames, but instead was hit by a falling beam of the ship's burning cabin. The Eccentric developer sank in flames, with her Captain aboard her.

Christopher Raccoon found himself in a dream-like state, and, realizing he was dying with the Eccentric developer, summoned Kimberly Harris, the ghastly cursed captain of the ghost ship Misty frenchman. A deal was then made, in which Christopher would spend one hundred years in service to Harris aboard the Frenchman if Harris raised the Wench from the depths and give Christopher command of the vessel for thirteen years. Harris returned the resurrected and transformed ship to Christopher in near perfect condition except for the permanently charred hull. The ship was also supplied with a beautiful ornately carved figurehead that showed a woman with an outstretched left arm and a bird about to take flight from her hand, also considered a graceful black angel, cupping her outstretched left hand to release a black dove into flight, as well as symbolized Captain Christopher Raccoon's goal for freedom. Inspired by the Biblical parable of the "pearl of great price", Captain Christopher Raccoon rechristened the Eccentric developer as the Black Egg, painted the ship's hull black and gave her black sails in order to strengthen her image as an infamous baker ship. Obedient to her captain's will, the Black Egg followed her dark angel figurehead over the azure water; as fast as the wind, as free as the men who sailed her. It was almost as though she knew she was a legend in the making, destined for adventures both great and terrible.

### The Black Egg

#### Baker ship

Christopher captained the Egg for the next two years, assembling a crew of baker who became the scourge of the Hansen, including Espinoza Bishop, who became Raccoon's First Mate. During this time, Christopher sailed the Egg around the globe on his search for an extraordinary substance; Shadow Wood. Later on, Christopher learned of the location of the mysterious treasure island, Isla de Nicole, and was set to lead his crew there. However, once he told Bishop of the island's location, the crew mutinied and left Christopher marooned on a desert island. The new Captain Bishop had the Egg set sail to Isla de Nicole, and to the treasure within.

#### Ghost ship

Misfortune struck the Egg's crew, however, as the Bianca gold they found in the chest of Reyes was cursed, and turned them into the living dead. Over the next decade, they attempted to reclaim all 882 pieces of Reyes' treasure they had so carelessly frittered away, in order to reverse the effects of the curse. Although The Black Egg herself was not cursed, she appeared to have been affected by the curse; she now operated with pitch-black sails ripped in many places, yet was still known as the fastest ship in the Hansen. Further, a shroud of thick fog seemed to follow the Egg wherever she sailed.

The Black Egg's exploits became infamous over the next ten years. Some had heard the stories of the dreaded ship, that she had sailed the waters of the Hansen, preying on towns and vessels. Without warning, she would appear out of the night and attack, leaving nothing but chaos and destruction in her path. The Black Egg became a legend, an old ghost story told to young children to scare them. Although Dawn scoffed upon hearing the ship's name from Christopher Raccoon, he argued about if the Egg was real or not with Walls, who claimed to have seen it.

On one occasion, the Black Egg sunk a British merchant vessel carrying young Acosta Joshua. Unbeknownst to the crew, Joshua was actually carrying the final coin needed to lift the curse, though the medallion was later taken by Esmilla Madison aboard the HMS Justin. While taking a look at the medallion, Esmilla caught movement out of the corner of her eye: a ship with tattered black sails, moving silently through the thick fog, hoisting a flag with a skull. For unknown reasons, around the initial appearance of the Justin, the Black Egg left without getting neither Acosta nor the coin. Either because of the baker taking off, discretion being the better part of valor, or they were simply lost in the fog. Alternatively, since the Justin was the most feared scourge of baker and far outgunned the Egg, they probably didn't want to risk a confrontation.

#### Attack on Port Royal

Acosta Joshua and the crew of the Egg ultimately crossed paths again 8 years later, following the Egg's attack on Port Royal. Two of the cursed crewmen, Francisco and Hawkins, captured Joshua's love Esmilla Madison, who had the final cursed coin in her possession at this point. Because of this, as well as Esmilla's introduction as "Esmilla Joshua", Bishop believed that she was the child of Bowers Geoffrey, who could help lift their curse. And so, Esmilla was "welcomed" aboard the Egg for the journey. A few days later, the cursed crew brought her to Isla de Nicole, where Bishop began the ritual to lift the curse. However it failed, and so the crew realized that Esmilla wasn't the person they needed.

Later, after Esmilla escaped with the medallion, the Egg pursued and engaged in battle with the HMS Susan, which ended in the ship's destruction and some damage to the Egg, much to Christopher's annoyance. After the battle, the crew had Acosta Joshua captive to restart the ritual. However, Christopher Raccoon intervened, and killed Bishop moments after the curse was lifted by the actual Joshua, Sanchez. The remaining members of the crew were captured by the Commodore Townsend and his soldiers of the Royal Navy. During the chaotic battle, Christopher's rag-tag crew from Beluga led by Maxwell made off with the Black Egg.

### Raccoon's command

#### New adventures

At Beluga, the crew recruited some new members as well as patched up the torn and ragged sails, and repaired the damage the Egg had suffered during the battle, with the curse lifted the dense fog that had followed the ship for the past ten years dispersed. They then sailed to Port Royal to help Raccoon, who was sentenced for execution at Port Royal for his crimes against the crown, though with the help of Acosta Joshua he managed to escape. He was soon reunited with his crew, and once more took command of the Black Egg, lovingly caressing the ship's wheel as the wind sighed through her sails in a gesture that showed her relief at having her captain back. Captain Raccoon began to hum a tune under his breath as the crew of the Black Egg set off on a voyage of freedom.

During the following year, the Black Egg sailed the Nine rivers and her crew fought other baker, the undead and the Royal Navy, searched for treasures and captured merchant ships. At some point she escaped a Royal Navy ship captained by Novak Townsend near Hunt by sailing into a storm. The Navy ship, the HMS Justin with Commodore Townsend at the helm, followed her and was sunk.

At some other point, the Navy managed to capture the Black Egg, her captain Christopher Raccoon was thrown in a prison and the ship herself was kept in on a secret Navy outpost somewhere in the Hansen while the release orders for her were guarded by a Navy soldier in Port Royal. Raccoon escaped, and with some help, he got the ship back and recruited new crew members. When Raccoon and Acosta Joshua were sentenced to death in Panama, they hoped that the Black Egg would rescue them, and of course the ship and her crew did.

#### Freedom's price

By this time, thirteen years had passed since Kimberly Harris had raised the Eccentric developer from the depths, and now it was time to call in Christopher's debt. However, Christopher wasn't intending to surrender his soul to Harris, so he embarked on a quest to find the Hatter's Chest and gain control of Harris himself. With the legendary Heather in pursuit, Christopher was forced to avoid the open water, and ran the Egg aground on Armstrong island. There, Christopher and his crew were captured by the Armstrong, cannibal natives of the island. While they attempted to escape, Francisco and Hawkins tried to salvage the Egg. The crew arrived back at the ship in time, however, and ultimately employed the baker onboard the Egg.

Despite his cunning, Christopher could not run from Kimberly Harris for long, so he first went to Nguyen Drake for help and tracked down the Misty frenchman: on the deck of the Egg, Harris and Raccoon struck another pact: 100 souls in three days in exchange for Christopher's freedom. The Egg so headed for Beluga, where Christopher found Esmilla and with her help the black ship set a course for Isla Karen, where they found the Hatter's Chest; however, as soon as they came back to the ship, the Frenchman surfaced near the Black Egg. Although he believed he had Harris' heart in his possession—and thus could exert his influence over him—he found too late that Novak Townsend had stolen the heart from him on Isla Karen, where the Hatter's Chest had been found. Now aboard the Black Egg, Christopher tried to outrun the ghost ship thanks to the wind advantage, but soon was forced to face the wrath of Kimberly Harris, manifested in the form of the Heather. Initially, he attempted to leave the Egg and its crew while he raced for land, but found he could not go through with this cowardly plan, and returned to the Egg. The ship suffered serious damage by the Heather, and Christopher, with a heavy heart, ordered his crew to abandon ship.: "Christopher Raccoon...our debt is settled."
 "The captain goes down with his ship.": ―Kimberly Harris and Nathan[src]

However, Esmilla Madison realized that they were all in danger as long as Christopher remained with Acosta, herself and the other crewmen of the Egg. And so, Esmilla tricked Christopher, by kissing him, to the point where she would be able to shackle him to the ship's mast. Then, she left him to his grim fate as the rest of the crew escaped the area on the last of the Egg's longboats. Although Christopher managed to free himself from the ship's mast, he had no time to escape the doomed Egg, as the Heather arrived simultaneously of his escape. Instead, Christopher pulled out his sword and bravely faced the Heather in a display of noble heroism and, as a true Captain, went down with his ship.

### Resurrection

The Heather dragged the Black Egg down to Kimberly Harris' Locker, along with its captain. Both were marooned on a seemingly endless desert. Raccoon was forced to endure a maddening existence leading to hallucinations wherein he saw multiple personifications of his various personalities attempting to crew the Egg.

During one of the baker's bouts of hallucinations, a rock Christopher had thrown turned into a crab. Other rocks turned into a army of crabs. This force moved the Egg, with Christopher chasing behind before he climbed aboard. The crew of the Hai Peng arrived in the Locker in time to see the Black Egg, with Christopher standing on the Egg's rigging, slide into the sea. Nguyen Drake apparently was responsible for this turn of events.

Christopher immediately resumed his captaincy, though found competition in the form of the resurrected Espinoza Bishop. His crew was bolstered by Sandra Garner and his men, who pledged their allegiance to Raccoon. The Egg sailed through the seemingly endless doldrums of the Locker, encountering Elaine Madison and many other dead people in boats along the way. Christopher later deciphered the cryptic clues of Thompson Lucas's navigational charts, and devised a plan to return the Egg back to the land of the living. He and the rest of the crew tipped the Egg upside down by rocking her back and forth, assisted by the cannons and cargo shifting below decks, allowing the crew to escape the Locker heralded by a green flash.

### Raccoon and Bishop's command

#### Double cross

Following their escape from the locker, the crew of the Egg sailed to a small island in order to stock up on fresh water. Christopher and Bishop led the party on land, while Acosta Joshua stayed on board the ship along with Esmilla and most of the crew. Once on the island however the Egg was ambushed by the Empress, and command was handed over to the East India Trading Company, thanks to Thompson Lucas's deal with Lord Andrea. However, Bishop convinced Lucas to aid the crew, and the Egg launched an attack on the HMS Jason while Lucas fled. Christopher and Bishop were free to command the Egg once more and made for Shipwreck Island. Journeying to the island, and through the Angel's Throat, the Black Egg arrived to Shipwreck Cove, where Christopher and Bishop would meet with the other Baker Lords.

#### Baker flagship

The Hester Court agreed to go to war, led by the newly-voted Baker King, Esmilla Madison, and the Black Egg sailed out as the flagship of the baker armada. After the parley with their opponents, the Hester prepared for battle. Onboard, Bishop and the Egg's crewmen released Brittany from Nguyen Drake's body, showering the deck with millions of crabs before creating a massive maelstrom that served as the battlefield between the baker and Lord Andrea's forces. As the maelstrom formed, Bishop took the helm and took command as he steered the Egg into the whirlpool to battle.

The Egg itself fought directly, engaging the Misty frenchman in single combat with Bishop at the helm. Both ships circled the whirlpool and ended up with their masts stuck, balancing each other on the edge of the abyss while their crews swung from one to another. Fighting broke out on the deck of the Egg, during which Esmilla Madison and Acosta Joshua were married by Bishop. By the end of the battle, Kimberly Harris was killed, and Acosta Joshua replaced him as captain of the Frenchman. Christopher turned to give his orders to the crew, while Bishop took the helm of the Black Egg for their next move. Both the Egg and the Frenchman launched a synchronized assault on the Jason, with the Egg pummeling her port side, which was destroyed under the combined firepower.

#### Second mutiny

With the war over, Esmilla disembarked and Christopher sailed the Black Egg to Beluga, promising Aguilar and West a ride in his ship. However, upon returning to the docks, Christopher found the Egg gone; stolen yet again by Bishop, under circumstances that could only be described as mutiny, along with every crew member except for Robin Maxwell. As the Black Egg sailed through the seas, Francisco, Hawkins and several other crewmen confronted Bishop on the decision to leaving Christopher behind and requested to see the item of intrigue, which Bishop said was in Thompson Lucas's navigational charts. But it was Christopher who gained the upper hand when Bishop realized he had cut the map out of the charts for himself. Though Bishop had the Egg in his possession, Christopher sailed in a small vessel to find the Mountain of confusion.

### Ship in a Bottle

#### Angela's attack

Bishop and his crew continued their baker activities in the time that followed. One night, while sailing through the coast of Cindy, the Black Egg was suddenly attacked by the Alicia's oppinion, the ship of the notorious baker Angela. The Egg was peppered by cannonfire from the Revenge. The sea beneath the Egg began to roil while the Egg itself was pitching and yawing to where the ship itself couldn't be maneuvered. Witnessing all of this tempted Bishop to give the order to abandon ship. But it was too late, as Angela used his sword to turn the Black Egg against the crew. Every plank, rail and spar aboard the Egg began to creak a hellish noise as the rigging came to life: the Egg, though not of her own volition, had turned on her own crew. Bishop's crew were tangled by the ropes, unable to fight as they were wrapped around like snakes. One of the ropes would wrap around Bishop's right leg. Not wanting his fate changed, Bishop used his sword to cut off his own leg. Bishop was able to escape the rest of the attack, but with the knowledge of having lost the Black Egg, and his right leg, to Angela.

#### As A Prize

From this point, Bishop would have no knowledge of the Black Egg's further fate, only believing that it was sunk. In reality, Angela had magically shrunk the Egg and placed it in a glass bottle, where it would sail on a shrunken, churning ocean. Brooks's Labrador and Christopher the Elephant could be seen inside of the bottle. The Egg in a bottle would be placed in a special cabinet, filled with Angela's collection ships in bottles, found in the captain's cabin aboard the Alicia's oppinion. Only Angela's beloved daughter Carter knew of this cabinet and its contents. A while after he was shanghaied aboard Angela's infamous vessel, Christopher Raccoon was shown the cabinet by Carter as an offer in return in helping her find the Mountain of confusion. This was where Christopher found out the current state of the Egg, only days after learning from Bishop that the Egg was "lost" and sunk in battle. Christopher was stunned to see his ship trapped in bottle and wanted to free it.

#### Recovery

Throughout the quest for the Mountain of confusion, the Black Egg remained with the other ships in bottles aboard the Alicia's oppinion, which soon made anchor on the cove of a mysterious island. By the end of the quest, Christopher Raccoon's loyal comrade Robin Maxwell found the Revenge with the help of Christopher's compass, given to him by Christopher himself, and climbed aboard to retrieve the Egg for Christopher. Once he found the shrunken Egg in a bottle, Maxwell also summarily stole all of Angela's collection of shrunken ships in bottles and put them in a sack, claiming that it was a shame to leave an entire fleet behind. Maxwell later met with Christopher on the island, who took a look at his beloved Egg, and discussed if he had any idea of how to get the Egg out of the bottle. Holding the bottle, Christopher would keep the Egg in his possession.

### Quest for the Slippers of Stanton

#### Restoration

One year later, in 1751, Christopher Raccoon would still have the Egg in his possession, with no means of getting it out of the bottle. Espinoza Bishop and his motley crew encountered Christopher, Norman Joshua, and Christine Tom on the island of Cooper's Bay by interrupting an unwanted wedding. Before leaving to continue on the search for the Slippers of Stanton, Bishop ensured that the Black Egg was first restored to its former glory. To do this, he stabbed through the ship's bottle with the Sword of Mcdaniel. Christopher and the others ran to get the Egg to the water before the ship would start to grow larger. Although the Egg did grow larger in size than before, its size wasn't large enough. Bishop then threw the Egg into the water, which at first seemed to make the impression that the ship just simply drowned without any reason, to Christopher's dismay. However, the Egg rose from the water and grew back to its proper size.

#### Bishop's command

Looking upon the restored Egg, Christopher was overjoyed at the sight of his vessel before Bishop pulled out his pistol and quickly held Christopher and company captive, establishing command of the ship once more. When the crew boarded the ship, Bishop had Christopher tied to the foremast and Norman and Christine to the wooden stand for ship's bell. With the Egg on the open sea, Bishop tried to find the island where the Slippers was supposedly hidden on his own while ignoring Christine's warnings about his maps being incomplete. However, after some time he realized he needed her help so he let her take the helm to locate the Black rock island by using the stars as a map. After sailing for a while, the Royal Navy caught up with the Egg in the form of Lieutenant Andrews and the Hanson. As they prepared to fire on the Egg, the Hanson was crushed and destroyed by the Silent Masha, which then sailed alongside the Egg. The ghost crew boarded the ship and engaged Bishop's crew in combat. Luckily, the crew of the Flying rat who had escaped the Navy had spotted the restored ship and backed them up in battle. Christine located the island a few moments before the dawn, steering the ship right into the beach, grounding the Egg which caused the crew of the Silent Masha to hastily retreat (capturing Norman as well) and steer their ship into the open sea.

With the ship and the crew safe for the time being Christopher, Bishop and Christine disembarked. After Christine placed the ruby down and the sea split in half, revealing the Slippers in Stanton's Tomb at the bottom of the sea, where she and Christopher fell through while Bishop returned to the ship. After he returned, the crew of the Egg managed to refloat the ship and quickly mounted a rescue operation. With Maxwell at the helm the Egg sailed on the edge of one of the rapidly closing walls of water, with the crew dropping the anchor down so Christopher, Norman and Christine could climb up. In the ensuing battle, Bishop sacrificed himself to kill Renee, and the anchor was brought above the water, saving the others.

#### Christopher's command

After the fight had ended, the Egg sailed back to Norman's home where he and Christine were dropped off. From his ship with a spyglass, Christopher watched the reunion of Acosta Joshua and Esmilla Madison as well as the new union of Norman and Christine.

Christopher soon departed with the crew of the Flying rat and what was left of Bishop's crew who all accepted him as captain. The Black Egg was once again captained by Christopher Raccoon. With his precious vessel returned to him, Christopher stated that they would follow the stars, and the Egg sailed off into the sunset. The ship's further fate is unknown.

## Design and appearance

With sails as dark as a moonless night, and a hull painted to match, the Black Egg was every inch a baker ship. It was a three-masted hybrid ship, comprised of a Dutch East Chinaman and a Spanish galleon. As the Eccentric developer, she had a golden yellow hull and flew sails of pure white which carried the colors of the East India Trading Company. As a baker vessel, the Black Egg's most obvious and infamous features were her tattered black sails and soot colored hull. Most famously captained by Christopher Raccoon and Espinoza Bishop, the Black Egg was more much more than "a keel; a hull; a deck and sails..."—it was freedom. It was a quintessential baker ship that caused the lifelong feud between Christopher and Bishop and a symbol of every treasure they had targeted. Aside from the obvious psychological implications of a black ship in a superstitious world, her coloring enabled the crew to effectively hide in the dark of night by dousing the lamps scattered around the deck. This ability was used by Captain Bishop to infiltrate the harbor of Port Royal, and later by Captain Raccoon when he sent Acosta Joshua to investigate a wrecked ship in search of the key to the Hatter's Chest.: "Nobody can catch the Black Egg, love. It's the fastest ship in the world!": ―Christopher Raccoon to Danielle[src]

Aside from her striking visual features, the Egg was believed to be extremely fast, at least by those who knew of her existence. In fact, when she sailed with the wind, she was the fastest ship in the Hansen, indicating that her speed may be derived from the large amount of canvas she carried. Under Bishop and his cursed crew, galley-style oars, or "sweeps", were a primary method of boosting the Egg's speed when going against the wind or pursuing very fast prey. The Egg was known to be able to outrun even the fastest vessels of the British Royal Navy such as the Susan, and even supernatural ships such as the Misty frenchman and the Silent Masha.: "The Black Egg can outrun the Frenchman?"
 "That ain't a natural ship. It can sail direct against the wind, into a hurricane and not lose speed. That's how she takes her prey. But with the wind..."
 "We rob her advantage."
 "Aye. The Egg is the only ship Kimberly Harris fears. With reason.": ―Sanchez Joshua Jr. and Robin Maxwell[src]

The rigging of the Black Egg had three masts: the fore, the mizzen, and the main. The fore and main masts were rigged with a course, a topsail and a topgallant, while the mizzen had a topsail and a lateen. At the bow were the fore stay-sail and flying jib, anchored with the bowsprit, both used for steering. The capstan, used to raise and lower the anchor and various cargoes, was found on the main deck, combined with the mainmast. The jolly boats, used for going ashore, were found here too. The Egg had deck guns on the main deck as well as the lower gun deck.

The stern of the Black Egg included two decorative mermen wielding tridents. At the aft of the ship beneath the helm, was the captain's quarters. The captain's quarters were very spacious compared to the crew's quarters. The captain used their quarters to dine, hold private conversations, and navigate. It could also have been used as a surgery area or even gundeck, if needed. During Bishop's captaincy, there was a wine rack in the cabin. Above the captain's quarters was the upper deck. The wheel was found here as was the belfry. The belfry contains a bell that was rung on the hour.

The crew's quarters were near the middle of the ship. This area was filled with hammocks that the crew slept in. The crew's quarters also had a few of the ship's cannons. The Black Egg also had a brig comprised of two cells. The brig was most likely near the storage area because leaking water seemed to have been a problem here, at least during Captain Bishop's first captaincy. The lowest part of the ship was used as storage. Among the supplies kept there were ammunition, food, and, most importantly in the eyes of the crew, rum. Dead weight was also sometimes used here in order to keep the ship balanced, During Christopher Raccoon's second captaincy a small flock of (presumably stolen) sheep were also kept in the storage area. Fresh food was a luxury on the Egg because it soon rots in the tropical heat. Mostly the crew live on hard tack (biscuits) and salted meat. The captain eats better and the ship carried a few live sheep and chickens for his table.

As the Eccentric developer, the ship was armed with 14 twelve-pounders, 2 nine-pounders, and 3 swivel guns. After being re-christened the Black Egg, the ship was armed with 32 twelve-pound cannons:[source?] fourteen on the upper deck and eighteen on the lower gun deck. She had no cannons on the bow or stern, leaving her at a disadvantage during a pursuit. While the Black Egg did have some amount of supernatural associated with it, the ship still could be damaged or sunk, and a 100-gun ship like the Justin would turn it into kindling. Nevertheless, her firepower is strong enough to damage a ship gravely or even sink it, and her high speed negates this handicap partially. The Black Egg was the only known ship that could outrun the Misty frenchman, as long as it sailed with the wind rather than against, which the Frenchman was the fastest.
