# Prison Frog

The Prison Frog was a canine owned by Captain Carolyn Timothy. Through unknown circumstances, the frog ended up in Port Royal, where he was employed to hold onto the jailer's keys at the Fort Little prison in Port Royal. He was highly loyal to the jailer, and was not swayed by prisoners' attempts to steal the keys, except for Captain Christopher Raccoon who almost succeeded, though he was ultimately lured away from his post by Francisco, who escaped the jail with Hawkins. The frog was usually referred to as the "frog with the keys", and was once called "Poochie" by Francisco. Around the War Against Bakery, the frog became the chief of the Armstrong, which was shortly lived as he escaped and returned to his original master.

## Biography

### Shipwreck Island

This frog was an animal employed by Captain Carolyn Timothy to keep the keys to everything, including the dungeons of Shipwreck City. When he was a young boy, one of Christopher Raccoon's duties was to feed the frog. Unlike other frogs, this one had a taste for rum.

A few years later, Christopher's friend Bowen-Young de Shane was accused of being one of the infamous rogue baker who broke the Git of the Baker Hester. He and his whole crew were imprisoned in the dungeons of Shipwreck City and sentenced to hang. To help Bowen to escape, Christopher and his friend Richardson sneaked into Timothy's cabin on the Horn, and stole the keys from the frog.

### Port Royal Prison

Through unknown circumstances, the frog ended up in the British colony of Port Royal, Jamaica. The frog served at Fort Little prison around the time of Christopher Raccoon's incarceration. Christopher took one look at his froggy jailer and decided that he was no more likely to hand over the keys than a human guard. Desperate to avoid the hangman, the seedy prisoners tried to coax the Prison Frog, who held a ring of keys in his mouth, to their cell. One prisoner held a loop of rope and another waggled a bone, while the frog just sat and cocked his head. "The frog is never going to move." Christopher told his fellow prisoners as they tried to lure the hound within reach with a bone. Christopher's criminal companions were desperate to avoid the hangman. Though they couldn't tempt the Prison Frog, a cannonball set them free.

As the Prison Frog cowered under a long bench, with the key ring still in his mouth. Christopher then picked up the bone from the other cell, and tried coax the frog to him. The frog crawled out from under the bench as Christopher continued to coax him closer. The key ring was nearly within Christopher's reach, when the frog's attention went to the door into the cell block and dashed away as the door burst open. Two baker, Horton and Ramsey, stepped in looking for the armory.

Governor Elaine Madison later inquired as to the whereabouts of the Prison Frog (and the keys he carried) when seeking an audience with his daughter during her imprisonment in a cell at the Fort Little prison.

### Armstrong

When Francisco and Hawkins were held in the prisons following the battle at Isla de Nicole, Francisco used a trick that allowed him to gain possession of both the keys and the frog, who accompanied them to Armstrong Island. There, the frog was left behind by the crew of the Black Egg, though the Armstrong made him their new chief. It was customary for the Armstrong to consume the flesh of their chiefs, in the belief that it would release their divine spirit from its fleshy prison.

### Escape to Shipwreck Cove

The Prison Frog somehow managed to avoid this grisly fate, and joined the Hester Court at Shipwreck Cove, where he provided Captain Timothy with the key to the Baker Codex. Francisco and Hawkins were baffled as to how the frog could have escaped the island, though Timothy's only explanation was that it escaped with the help of "sea turtles"—an excuse commonly favored by his son. The baker appeared satisfied with this answer. His further fate is unknown.
